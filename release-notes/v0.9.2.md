# Denon AVR Plus 0.9.2

**LFE that works, center level and All Zone Stereo the way Denon documents
them, Zone 2 that says when it cannot play an input, and installation from
the Marketplace.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. After updating, reload the Home
Assistant app or page once, so it shows the new texts.

---

## New

- **Zone 2 tells you when it cannot play an input.** Zone 2 on many
  receivers plays no HDMI input; an AVR-X2600H stays silent and keeps its
  source. When Zone 2 does not switch, Home Assistant now shows an error and
  leaves that input out of the Zone 2 list until it restarts. To hide such
  inputs for good, take them out of the Zone 2 list under Configure →
  Sources.
- **Diagnostics with the last 300 telnet lines** sent to and received from
  the receiver, so a problem can be read from what the receiver actually
  said. Download them right after the problem shows.
- **Installation from the Marketplace** of Home Assistant 2026.11 (Settings
  → Marketplace → Add from link); the README describes both the Marketplace
  and HACS for older versions.

## Fixed

- **LFE level** can be set. It was sent with a sign (`PSLFE -1`), which the
  receiver ignores; it now goes out as two digits (`PSLFE 01` is −1 dB), as
  every Denon document gives it.
- **Center level adjust** is asked as `PSCLV`, the command in Denon's 2020
  table and the function the receiver itself calls Center Level Adjust.
  Receivers from before 2016 keep `PSDIL`.
- **All Zone Stereo** is asked as `MNZST?` without a space, as Denon writes
  it; with the space receivers did not answer.
- A sound mode the receiver lists twice is offered once (an AVR-X2600H
  listed Stereo four times).
- IMAX offers On as well as Auto and Off (Denon's 2020 table); the LFC
  containment amount starts at 1.

## Receiver test

- Input mode: an input with HDMI only keeps HDMI whatever is sent; the test
  reports this as no effect.
- The recordings of an AVR-X2600H with Dolby Digital 5.1 confirm Dynamic
  Compression, Loudness Management and switching Auto Lip Sync.

## Documentation

- The protocol pages compare Denon's 2020 and 2022 tables with the 2015
  document and with what an AVR-X2600H answered.

## Tested

- 647 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, strict typing,
  the coverage per module and the repository checks pass.
- Receiver test runs on an AVR-X2600H (firmware 3.139.175) with PCM and with
  Dolby Digital 5.1. `PSCLV` and `MNZST?` follow Denon's tables and are not
  yet confirmed on a receiver.
