# Denon AVR Plus 0.7.2

**Test the receiver, redone: a start page, a page per step with what runs
right now, one progress, a result titled by what came back, and settings
tested with what they need.** From the second test on an AVR-X2000.

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. After updating, reload the Home
Assistant app or page once: an app that stays open keeps the texts of the
old version, which shows as empty menu entries and bare keys.

---

## Changed

- **Start page**: the three steps, how long each takes, what changes, and
  that a main zone that is off is turned on for the test. **Start** begins;
  the repair for a model not checked yet opens the same page.
- **A page per step** ("Step 2 of 3") with a checklist of the steps, the
  setting being tested right now, and one progress from 0 to 100 % over the
  whole test instead of one per step. Closing the window stops the test and
  puts back what it changed.
- **The result** is titled "Everything put back", or "Check the receiver"
  with the settings that did not come back. It counts questions answered,
  commands confirmed, a missing precondition, no effect, and skipped.
- **Settings that need another one** are tested with it: bass and treble
  with tone control on for the moment, contrast, brightness, saturation,
  enhancer and noise reduction in the custom picture mode; both go back
  afterwards. Audyssey settings with MultEQ off and Zone 2 settings with
  Zone 2 off count as a missing precondition instead of no effect. The
  diagnostics record the sound mode, MultEQ, tone control, picture mode,
  input and Zone 2 at the start of the test.

## Tested

- 515 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, the typing
  baseline, the coverage floors, the translation, locale-slug and icon
  checks pass.
- New: every flow screen a test reaches is checked in all eight languages
  (title, description, field labels and help, menu entries, progress, abort
  and error texts, and the placeholders they use).
- On a receiver: in the AVR-X2000's quiet test, tone control, auto lip sync,
  restorer, volume scale, muting level, subwoofer mode, LPF for LFE, sleep
  timer, audio delay, picture mode, aspect ratio and the volume were
  confirmed. Bass, treble and the picture adjustments had no effect because
  tone control was off and the picture mode not custom; that is what the
  new preconditions handle.
