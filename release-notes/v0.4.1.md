# Denon AVR Plus 0.4.1

**The tuner as entities, zone 2 with its own media browser, quick selects in
the source menu, and the signal of an input on the media player.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. Nothing to change when coming
from 0.4.0.

---

## New

### Tuner

- **Tuner preset** as a select (01–56; A1–G8 on receivers on port 80) and
  the **FM frequency** in MHz as a number that tunes directly. Both are
  available while a zone that is on plays the tuner: the receiver takes
  tuner commands only then.
- **`denonavr_plus.store_tuner_preset`** stores the station that plays as
  a preset. Off the tuner it says so instead of sending a command the
  receiver would ignore.

### Media player

- **Zone 2 browses and plays** its own quick selects (`Z2QUICK`, numbered,
  where the receiver answers), its sources and the tuner presets. Web
  addresses stay on the main zone; zone 2 hears them by joining it.
- **Quick selects in the source menu** of the main zone, by their names,
  after the inputs. A quick select named like an input stays out of the
  menu; the browser still has it.
- **The signal of an input** below its name: decoder and sample rate, such
  as `DOLBY TRUEHD · 48 kHz`.
- **Volume step** per zone in the options (0.5 to 5 dB) for volume up and
  down. Empty keeps the receiver's 0.5 dB.
- **Main zone off puts the receiver in standby**, an option under Zones:
  turning the main zone off then puts the whole receiver in standby
  (`PWSTANDBY`), so no other zone keeps playing.
- Picking a **source or a sound mode** turns a zone in standby on first.
- Browser items carry their own **media class**, so quick selects, inputs
  and tuner presets get their own icons.

## Changed

- A quick select number the zone does not have is refused with a message.

## Tested

- 422 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, the typing
  baseline, the coverage floors, the translation, locale-slug and icon
  checks pass. Each change has a test that fails without it.
- The entity snapshot of the AVC-X3700H gains the tuner preset select and
  the tuner frequency number.
- Not tried on a receiver yet: recalling, storing and tuning on the tuner
  (`TPAN`, `TPANMEM`, `TFAN`), zone 2 quick select from the browser, and
  `PWSTANDBY` from the option.
