# Denon AVR Plus 0.5.0

**HEOS music in the browser, announcements that put the receiver back, voice
search, a Music switch per zone for Music Assistant, artwork for inputs, and
blueprints with an example dashboard.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. Nothing to change when coming
from 0.4.1.

---

## New

### HEOS music (receivers with HEOS)

- The browser's **HEOS** folder holds the receiver's music sources: HEOS
  favorites, local media servers and USB, TuneIn, playlists, history and
  signed-in services, in pages of 50.
- Albums and playlists play as a whole, **now, next, at the end or
  replacing the queue** (`enqueue` of `play_media`); tracks and stations
  play alone.
- The **HEOS favorites** follow the quick selects in the source menu.
  `play_media` takes a favorite or a quick select by name or number
  (`media_content_type: favorite`, `media_content_id: Bayern 3`).
- Favorites need the HEOS system signed in to a HEOS account, which the
  HEOS app normally has done. This integration asks for no account.

### Announcements

- Text to speech and other media played with `announce` (voice
  assistants, `tts.speak`) play over HEOS at the **announcement volume**
  (the call's `extra: {volume: 0.5}`, or a new option of the main zone).
  Power, input, volume and mute are put back once it has played. Music that
  played over HEOS before does not resume.
- **`denonavr_plus.snapshot`** and **`denonavr_plus.restore`** keep and put
  back what a zone does, for your own sequences.

### Media player and zones

- **Voice**: "play Kino on the receiver" works with Assist; the player
  searches quick selects, inputs and HEOS favorites by name.
- **Artwork for inputs**: an input without cover art gets its own picture,
  a colour and a pictogram per kind of input, which the media control card
  paints as its background.
- **Music switch** per zone, for Music Assistant and automations: on turns
  the zone on and to the network input; off turns it off only while it
  still plays the network, so it never ends a film.

### Documentation

- **Blueprints**: TV on, receiver on; night mode.
- **An example dashboard** from the standard cards and card features.
- **With Music Assistant and the HEOS integration**: which integration does
  what, so nothing is controlled twice.

## Changed

- Announcements follow how HEOS switches streams (as Music Assistant's HEOS
  provider handles it): a short stop while it switches is no end.
- TuneIn is listed even when it reports itself unavailable, as the HEOS
  integration does.
- HEOS playback errors appear in the diagnostics.

## Tested

- 443 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, the typing
  baseline, the coverage floors, the translation, locale-slug and icon
  checks pass. Each change has a test that fails without it; the blueprints
  are filled and validated as automations.
- The entity snapshot of the AVC-X3700H gains the two Music switches.
- Not tried on a receiver yet: HEOS browsing, favorites, queueing and
  announcements (they follow the HEOS CLI specification and the HEOS
  integration), and `Z2SOURCE` for zone 2 in a group.
