# Denon AVR Plus 0.3.2

**Zone 2 is a device of its own with its own room, the setup sub-devices
follow the receiver's room, and three things seen on an AVR-X2600H are
fixed: the zone 2 source, MultEQ options, and the rooms of the
sub-devices.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. Coming from 0.3.1: give the new
zone device its room. [Upgrade notes →](../docs/UPGRADING.md#from-031)

---

## Changed

- **Zone 2 and zone 3 are devices of their own**, for example
  `AVR-X2600H Zone 2`, with the zone's media player, volume and source.
  A zone usually plays in another room, so its device keeps a room of its
  own. Entity ids stay the same. The "turned on while unreachable" trigger
  is offered by each zone device for its own player.
- **Audio, Video, Picture, Speakers and Graphic EQ are in the receiver's
  room.** A sub-device without a room takes it, and those in the
  receiver's room move along when the receiver moves. One you put in
  another room stays there. Before, their entities were missing from the
  room.

## Fixed

- **Zone 2 source.** Zone 2 showed the source `QUICK0`: the answer to the
  quick select query was taken for a source, and so were other zone
  settings. Only a source code is a source now; a source reported before
  the receiver's source list is known still counts.
- **MultEQ options.** The select offered Manual, which an AVR-X2600H does
  not have. Where the receiver lists fewer options than the protocol knows,
  the select offers the receiver's.

## Confirmed on a receiver

The capability check of an AVR-X2600H (firmware 3.139.175) is part of the
tests and the protocol docs now. It confirms the auto standby (`STBY`)
and Bluetooth transmitter (`BTTX`) formats. The model page lists what the
receiver answered, including the zone 2 channel levels, sleep timer, auto
standby and quick select.

## Tested

- 366 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, the typing
  baseline, the coverage floors, the translation, locale-slug and icon
  checks pass.
- Each fix has a test that fails without it; one test feeds every answer
  of the AVR-X2600H recording through the parser.
- The fixes follow the diagnostics of an AVR-X2600H; the new version has
  not been on a receiver yet.
