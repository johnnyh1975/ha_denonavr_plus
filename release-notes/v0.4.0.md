# Denon AVR Plus 0.4.0

**Media: a media browser, `play_media` with text to speech, zones as a
group, tuner presets, and now playing on receivers without HEOS. Zone 2
gets its own settings, and the AVR-X2000 its channel levels, picture
settings and network source.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. Nothing to change when coming
from 0.3.4. Coming from 0.3.1: [upgrade notes →](../docs/UPGRADING.md#from-031)

The new settings start disabled, like the other setup settings; enable them
on the device page.

---

## New

### Media

- **Media browser** on the main zone: the quick select presets, the
  sources, the tuner presets and, on receivers with HEOS, Home Assistant's
  media sources (text to speech, radio, …) on the first level.
- **`play_media`**: a quick select (`quick_select/2`), a source
  (`source/TUNER`) or a tuner preset (`tuner_preset/05`) on every receiver;
  a URL or text to speech over HEOS (`browse/play_stream`) on receivers with
  HEOS. Receivers without HEOS refuse a URL with a message. A zone in
  standby is turned on first.
- **Zones as a group.** Joining zone 2 to the main zone sets it to the
  main zone's source, so it follows every change of input; leaving keeps
  the input. Zone 2 then shows what the main zone plays and takes play,
  pause and skip.
- **The player names the input.** With nothing playing on the network, the
  title is the input and the line below it the sound mode, instead of an
  empty card.
- **Position, repeat and shuffle** from HEOS: the media player shows the
  playback position and the duration, and repeat and shuffle can be set.
- **Now playing without HEOS.** Receivers on port 80, such as the
  AVR-X2000, report title, artist, album and cover of the network source
  over telnet, and play, pause and skip reach them over telnet too.
- **Tuner**: station name or frequency as the title, the recalled preset,
  and skip steps through the presets. The receiver gives no preset names,
  so the browser lists them by number (01–56; A1–G8 on receivers on port
  80, as their documents name them).

### Receivers and zones

- **Zone 2 settings** on the zone 2 device, where the receiver answers them:
  sleep timer, front left and right level, quick select (as many presets as
  the main zone has) and auto standby. They answer on an AVR-X2000 and an
  AVR-X2600H; the AVR-X2000 has no zone 2 auto standby. A zone left out in
  the options gets none.
- **Channel levels on the AVR-X2000** and other receivers without the
  speaker calibration list: they come from the channel volume query; the
  configured speakers start enabled.
- **Picture settings on the AVR-X2000** in its own scale: contrast and
  saturation −6 to +6, brightness 0 to 12. Receivers on port 80 get these
  encodings; newer ones keep theirs.
- **Network source on the AVR-X2000.** It plays from the network as `NET`
  but did not list it; the source list now offers NETWORK.
- **Manufacturer and serial number** of receivers without the newer
  device information, read from the receiver's UPnP description.

### Diagnostics

- The **capability check** also reads the network screen text (`NSE`) and
  the network preset names (`NSH`), both read-only.
- The diagnostics show **how media reaches the receiver**: HEOS or telnet,
  which now-playing fields are filled, and the receiver's last answer to
  each HEOS command, such as playing a URL. Titles and the cover address are
  left out.

## Fixed

- **Sleep timer** was asked in a form neither the AVR-X2000 nor the
  AVR-X2600H answers, so it stayed unknown until it changed.
- **Zone 2 mute** was never asked and stayed unknown after a start.
- **Auto lip sync** is read from the HDMI setup list, where the AVR-X2000
  reports it. On the AVR-X2600H it stays unknown for now.

## Changed

- **Play, pause and skip** show only while a network source plays, not on
  a TV or disc input where they reached nothing.
- **Aspect ratio** shows 16:9 and 4:3, as the receiver calls them. The
  states stay `full` and `normal`, so automations keep working.

## Tested

- 412 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, the typing
  baseline, the coverage floors, the translation, locale-slug and icon
  checks pass.
- Each change has a test that fails without it; the zone 2 settings are
  checked against the capability checks of both receivers.
- Not tried on a receiver yet: playing a URL over HEOS, play, pause and
  skip over telnet, now playing over telnet, tuner presets, and the UPnP
  description on the AVR-X2000. The next capability check and diagnostics show the
  read-only parts.
