# Denon AVR Plus 0.9.4

**Every Denon and Marantz AV receiver from 2010 on, by its generation:
receivers from 2010 to 2012 can be added, Marantz gets Smart Select and
M-DAX right, and testing a receiver is optional.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. After updating, reload the Home
Assistant app or page once, so it shows the new texts.

---

## New

- **Receivers from 2010 to 2012 can be added** (AVR-1912, AVR-2112CI,
  AVR-2312CI, AVR-3311CI, AVR-3312, AVR-4311CI, AVR-4810, AVR-A100, Marantz
  NR1602, SR5006, SR6006, SR7005, AV7005 and their siblings). They have no
  device description, so the integration reads them the way the official
  integration does: model and serial number from the network description,
  inputs, their names and sound modes from the receiver's main zone page,
  the zones and every setting from its telnet answers. Their older value
  formats are used (Dynamic Volume Midnight/Evening/Day, restorer modes,
  presets A1–G8). Not yet tried on a receiver: reports are welcome.
- **Compatibility by generation.** The integration reads the receiver's
  generation, brand and kind of device from the receiver itself. A new page,
  [Compatibility](https://github.com/johnnyh1975/ha_denonavr_plus/blob/main/docs/COMPATIBILITY.md),
  lists the generations with example models, what differs between them and
  which models are tested.
- **Testing a receiver is optional.** Setup no longer asks to test a Denon or
  Marantz AV receiver, and no repair asks for it; Configure → Test the
  receiver runs it whenever you like. Setup offers it only for a device the
  integration does not describe (a network CD receiver, or a generation not
  seen yet). The test asks every question of every generation, and its
  result names the generation; so do the diagnostics.

## Fixed

- **Smart Select on Marantz.** Marantz receivers of interface level 0300 and
  newer (SR6011 on) call their
  presets Smart Select and take `MSSMART`/`Z2SMART`; the integration sent
  `MSQUICK`, which they ignore. Older Marantz receivers (NR1604, SR5008)
  keep Quick Select.
- **No restorer on Marantz.** Marantz receivers of level 0300 and newer list M-DAX
  under the name Restorer. The integration added a Restorer select that sent
  a command Marantz does not have, next to the M-DAX select. Only M-DAX is
  left; see [Upgrading](https://github.com/johnnyh1975/ha_denonavr_plus/blob/main/docs/UPGRADING.md).
- **Restorer on 2013 models outside the X series.** The AVR-1713, AVR-1913,
  AVR-2113CI and AVR-3313 take the restorer as Mode 1 to Mode 3, as their
  documents give it; they got the AVR-X2000's High/Medium/Low, which they do
  not know.
- **The lowest volume** of 2011 receivers (`MV99`) no longer reads as
  +19 dB.
- **A busy control connection** is explained better: the repair also names
  the Denon or Marantz AVR Remote app, which holds the connection while its
  tuner screen is open.

## Documentation

- Compatibility page; README, user guide and contributing guide follow the
  generations.
- The protocol pages describe the receivers' web interface, what the
  manufacturers' apps send, and the receivers before 2013.

## Tested

- 712 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, strict typing,
  the coverage per module and the repository checks pass.
- The changes follow the manufacturers' documents and apps and the recorded
  data of real receivers (from the official library's test data). The
  receivers from 2010 to 2012, the Marantz changes and the 2013 restorer are
  not yet confirmed on a receiver.
