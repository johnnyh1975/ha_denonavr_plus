# Denon AVR Plus 0.9.0

**Quality and documentation: a user guide with screenshots, a README that
compares Denon AVR Plus with the official integration, and every module
typed strictly and tested to 95 %.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. After updating, reload the Home
Assistant app or page once, so it shows the new texts.

---

## New

- **User guide** ([docs/USER_GUIDE.md](../docs/USER_GUIDE.md)): the receiver
  the way you use it, step by step and with screenshots: before you start,
  adding it, finding your way around, everyday use, the options and what to
  set them to, automations, the receiver test, moving over from another
  Denon integration, Music Assistant and troubleshooting.
- **README as the front page**: what Denon AVR Plus is, a comparison with
  the official Denon AVR Network Receivers integration across its
  functions, the supported receivers (now with the AVR-X2600H and the
  AVR-X2000 as tested), installation and first steps.
- **Reference** ([docs/REFERENCE.md](../docs/REFERENCE.md)): every option,
  entity, action and port, moved out of the README.
- **Contributing** ([CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md)): the receiver
  test, the receivers tested so far and what helps when setup fails.

## Fixed

- A sample rate the receiver reports as bare digits (an AVR-X2600H sends
  `441`) shows as 44.1 kHz instead of 441.
- The move offer and its repair showed a translation error in their
  title; they read "Take over from another integration?" and "Take over the
  receiver" now.
- The example dashboard shows the tuner only while it plays; its entities
  are unavailable otherwise and showed warnings.
- Port 23: the documentation no longer says both one and several telnet
  sessions. Plan for one connection per receiver until a receiver has been
  tested with two.

## Quality

- Strict typing: `mypy --strict` passes with nothing switched off.
- Every module is at 95 % test coverage or above; no module is held on a
  lower floor any more.
- The Home Assistant quality scale is met up to Platinum (self-assessed,
  as for every custom integration).
- An unused TCP client is removed.

## Tested

- 636 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, strict typing,
  the coverage per module and the repository checks pass.
- The screenshots come from Home Assistant 2026.10 talking to a simulated
  AVC-X3700H that answers with what real receivers sent.
