# Denon AVR Plus 0.8.0

**Moving over: take a receiver over from the official Denon integration or
from Denon AVR, with its entity IDs and history, and hand it back.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. After updating, reload the Home
Assistant app or page once, so it shows the new texts.

---

## New

- **Take a receiver over.** When you add a receiver that is already set up
  in the official Denon AVR Network Receivers integration (`denonavr`) or in
  Denon AVR (`denon_avr`), found by its address, MAC or serial number,
  Denon AVR Plus asks whether to take it over. While the other integration
  still runs the receiver later, a repair offers the same.
  - The other entry is disabled, not removed: many receivers accept one
    telnet session.
  - Its entities move here and keep their entity IDs, names, areas and
    history, so automations and dashboards keep working: from the official
    integration the media players of the zones, from Denon AVR (same unique
    IDs) every entity that Denon AVR Plus still has.
  - From the official integration, "Volume scale of the official
    integration" is switched on, so `volume_set` levels sound the same.
  - Automations and scripts using its actions, attributes or sound mode
    names (`denonavr.get_command`, `sound_mode_raw`, `MOVIE`, ...) are listed
    in a repair, each with what replaces it. Nothing in them is changed.
- **Undo the move** in the options: the entities go back with their entity
  IDs, the other entry is enabled again and Denon AVR Plus is disabled.

See [docs/UPGRADING.md](../docs/UPGRADING.md#coming-from-another-denon-integration).

## Tested

- 553 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, the typing

- The move and the way back are tested against Home Assistant's entity

  that runs one of them.
