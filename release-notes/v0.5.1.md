# Denon AVR Plus 0.5.1

**The HEOS queue, where HEOS music comes from, and HEOS playback errors as a
repair.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. Nothing to change when coming
from 0.5.0.

---

## New

- **The HEOS queue**, as the HEOS integration offers it: a Queue folder in
  the browser (an item plays from it), the media player's clear playlist,
  and the actions `denonavr_plus.get_queue`, `remove_from_queue` and
  `move_queue_item`. `play_media` with `media_content_type: queue` plays an
  item by its queue id.
- **Where it comes from**: the media player names the HEOS service it plays
  from (`app_name`, such as TuneIn or a media server) and the station of a
  radio (`media_channel`). A station counts as a channel, a song as music.
- **Playback errors** HEOS reports show as a repair, until something plays
  again.
- **Quick selects and favorites in the source menu** can be switched off
  (Sources options); on by default.

## Changed

- HEOS's "Url Stream" (a stream without metadata, such as an announcement)
  no longer shows as the title; the player names the input instead, as
  Music Assistant's HEOS provider handles it.

## Tested

- 449 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, the typing
  baseline, the coverage floors, the translation, locale-slug and icon
  checks pass. Each change has a test that fails without it.
- Not tried on a receiver yet: the queue commands and the service names
  (they follow the HEOS CLI specification).
