# Denon AVR Plus 1.0.1

**Fixes from a second independent review of 1.0.0: the volume ramp stops
when the zone changes elsewhere, the tuner waits for a waking receiver, and
settings read without telnet stay correct.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. Nothing to do when updating from
1.0.0.

---

## Fixed

- **The volume ramp stops when the zone changes elsewhere.** It kept
  stepping when the zone was switched off with the power switch or the
  remote, or its volume was set with the volume number or the receiver's
  knob; a zone switched on again later jumped to the ramp's level. It now
  stops then, and with an announcement or `denonavr_plus.restore`.
- **A tuner preset or frequency from standby** waits until the zone reports
  on before the input and the tuner command; a receiver that is still waking
  up drops them.
- **The sound mode list follows a genre switch** made within six seconds of
  the integration's own query for the list; it kept the old genre's modes
  until the next change.
- **Without telnet:** bass and treble become unknown when the receiver no
  longer offers tone control (Direct, say), instead of showing their old
  values; and a reading over the web interface that arrives after telnet is
  back no longer overwrites telnet's values.
- **Subwoofer:** the "only in Direct, Pure Direct or Stereo" reason now
  comes only when switching the subwoofer off.
- **The Zone 2 question** is removed with the receiver's entry.

## Documentation

- Input Mode: 1.0.0 said eARC is offered only where the receiver has it.
  It is left out where the receiver's own list leaves it out (AVC-8500H,
  SR6011); some receivers without eARC list it.
- The standby rule also covers the volume limit and scale, the speaker
  preset and the LFE low-pass; the reference says so.
- Video Mode Bypass and the 8K resolution are marked as not yet confirmed
  on a receiver.

## Tested

- 761 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, strict typing,
  the coverage per module and the repository checks pass. Each fix has a
  test that fails without it.
