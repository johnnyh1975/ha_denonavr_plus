# Denon AVR Plus 0.6.0

**Video and channel sensors, a firmware update sensor, Recover audio, and
the fixes from an independent review. Also everything of 0.5.1, which was
not published as a release of its own: the HEOS queue, the service and
station of HEOS music, and HEOS playback errors as a repair.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. Coming from 0.5.x: if you
imported the blueprint "TV on", [import it again](../docs/UPGRADING.md#from-05x).

---

## New

### Receiver data

- **Signal sensors** on receivers with the setup interface (not the
  AVR-X2000): input and output resolution and HDR, input color space,
  color depth and FRL rate on the Video device; the input signal's channels
  and the active speakers on the Audio device. Input resolution and HDR
  start enabled. Read at setup, with each poll, and a few seconds after an
  input, power or signal change.
- **Firmware update** (binary sensor, receivers with HEOS): whether HEOS
  reports an update, asked twice a day. HEOS gives no version; install it
  from the receiver's menu or the HEOS app.
- **Recover audio** (button, starts disabled): standby and back on with the
  input it had, for a receiver that has lost its sound.

### HEOS (from 0.5.1)

- **The HEOS queue**: a Queue folder in the browser, the media player's
  clear playlist, the actions `denonavr_plus.get_queue`,
  `remove_from_queue` and `move_queue_item`, and `play_media` with
  `media_content_type: queue`.
- The player names the **HEOS service** it plays from (`app_name`) and the
  **station** of a radio (`media_channel`).
- **Playback errors** HEOS reports show as a repair until something plays
  again; the repair goes with an unload or reload.
- **Quick selects and favorites in the source menu** can be switched off
  (Sources options).

## Fixed (independent review)

- **Media servers, USB sticks and input devices** under Local Music and AUX
  inputs open in the HEOS browser; they were dead entries.
- A **late HEOS answer** to a request that timed out could reach the next
  request (a wrong favorites list, a wrong station from the source menu).
  Requests now carry a sequence number and answers are matched on it.
- An **announcement that does not start** (HEOS reconnecting) left the zone
  on and loud; the zone is now put back at once, and without a HEOS link
  nothing is switched. A muted zone is unmuted for an announcement.
- **HEOS features** (announcements, queueing, clear playlist, transport)
  were offered on receivers whose HEOS does not answer, such as the 2015
  X-series; they now appear only while HEOS answers.
- The **turn-on volume** now applies however a zone is turned on: a
  source, `play_media`, a group or the Music switch, not only turn on.
- **Blueprint TV on**: a TV dropping off the network no longer turns the
  receiver on or off. Re-import the blueprint to get the fix.
- Queue actions on zone 2 say they belong to the main zone.
- "Url Stream" (a stream without metadata) is no title; the input shows
  instead.

## Tested

- 462 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, the typing
  baseline, the coverage floors, the translation, locale-slug and icon
  checks pass. Each fix has a test that failed without it.
- The entity snapshot of the AVC-X3700H gains the firmware update sensor
  and the Recover audio button.
- Not tried on a receiver yet: the signal sensors (they follow another
  integration that reads them on current AVR-X models), the update check,
  Recover audio, and the HEOS queue and browsing.
