# Denon AVR Plus 1.2.1

**Fixes from the diagnostics of an AVR-X2000 and an AVR-X2600H: the
subwoofer and speakers as the receiver reports them, bass and treble that
go below 0 dB, readable input names and the playing time without HEOS.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. After updating, reload the Home
Assistant app or page once. Read
[Upgrading](https://github.com/johnnyh1975/ha_denonavr_plus/blob/main/docs/UPGRADING.md):
input names and channel trims change.

---

## Fixed

- **A subwoofer reported as yes or no** (AVR-X2000, AVR-X2600H). The
  receiver answers `SSSPCSWF YES` or `NO`; *No* was taken as a subwoofer,
  so its trim stayed on, and the number of subwoofers showed nothing. It
  now offers *None* and *1* and sends yes or no.
- **Channel trims follow the speakers.** A speaker set up or removed on the
  receiver after setup now switches its trim on or off, once the receiver's
  answer is complete. Before, the trims were set once at setup and a
  removed center or surround kept its trim. Trims of speakers that did not
  change, and trims you disabled yourself, are left alone.
- **Bass and treble on receivers that publish no range** (AVR-X2000 and
  older) went from 0 to 100 dB, so they could not go below 0 dB. They now
  go from −6 to +6 dB in whole dB, as the protocol documents give them; the
  receiver ignored half steps.
- **The sound mode group** follows the receiver when it changes there; it
  kept the group read at the start.
- **Input names:** inputs the receiver lists without a name read as words:
  *Network*, *Tuner*, and on receivers before 2016 *Internet Radio*, *Media
  Server*, *iPod/USB* instead of NET, TUNER, IRADIO, SERVER, USB/IPOD.
  Choosing by the old name still works.
- **Playing time without HEOS** (AVR-X2000 and older): the elapsed time from
  the receiver's on-screen list is the player's position.
- A sound mode whose name starts with a digit (*7ch Stereo*) keeps its
  digit.

Found in an independent review before the release: quick changes of the
subwoofer, or a speaker's count reported on its own, could leave a speaker
counted as missing; names stored by an earlier start stayed in capitals;
the playing time ran on from the previous track.

## Tested

- 912 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, strict typing,
  the coverage per module and the repository checks pass. The speaker,
  input and playing time fixes run on lines recorded from an AVR-X2000 and
  an AVR-X2600H.
- Not yet tried on a receiver: setting the subwoofer to yes or no, bass and
  treble at −6 and +6 dB on an AVR-X2000, and whether the flagged sound mode
  always marks the current group.
