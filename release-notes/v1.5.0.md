# Denon AVR Plus 1.5.0

**The newest receivers' setup (settings per input, speaker layout with a way
back), settings the receiver grays out shown before you try, and fixes from
two independent reviews.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. After updating, reload the Home
Assistant app or page once. Read
[Upgrading](https://github.com/johnnyh1975/ha_denonavr_plus/blob/main/docs/UPGRADING.md):
grayed setup items are no longer *unavailable*.

---

## New

- **Newer receivers** (AVC-X3800H, X3900H and others of the Web Control
  generation), from LaserGuruGuy's recordings of an AVC-X3900H:
  - an **Inputs** device with the **4K/8K signal format** and **HDCP** per
    input; an input the receiver grays out is shown, not changed;
  - a **Speaker layout** select with an **Apply speaker layout** button. A
    new layout makes the receiver reset the speaker positions and front
    speaker A/B; a repair says how many and puts them back: first the
    layout, then each position you have not changed since. It keeps the way
    back over a restart of Home Assistant and over a second layout change;
  - **speaker connection** sensors per position (speakers and pre-out,
    pre-out only, none), the **subwoofer mode** and **subwoofer layout**.
  They start disabled; the Video and Speakers groups show them
  (Configure → Entities).
- **Settings the receiver grays out, before you try:**
  - an audio setting its menu shows grayed or hidden (`SSAUDSTS`) is refused
    with the reason before anything is sent;
  - an HDMI or video setup item the receiver grays out keeps its value, with
    the attributes `settable` and `own_setting`, instead of going
    *unavailable*.
- **Output color space and pixel depth** as signal sensors.
- The device page shows the **DTS decoder** next to the firmware and the
  **network module** as hardware.

## Changed

- **New installations start with fewer entities shown:**
  - the channel levels, with the rest of the speaker setup;
  - the Remote, whose on/off repeats the media player's power;
  - the Network Music switches, which appear once a player is linked to the
    network input.

  Installations you have keep their entities as they are.
- **Zone 2 inputs:** an input Zone 2 did not switch to now leaves the Zone 2
  list in the options only if the zone was on and the input did not come
  within ten seconds after all.
- The speaker layout repair shows its progress while it puts things back,
  which can take up to a minute.

## Fixed

Found in an independent architecture review, each reproduced first:
- an unexpected error while handling a line from the receiver ended the
  telnet connection for good, and a repair then blamed another program; the
  line is now logged and the connection goes on or reconnects;
- a stored input or sound mode the receiver no longer offers made the
  Sources or Sound modes step impossible to save;
- a Zone 2 input whose confirmation came late was removed from the options
  for good, although the zone had switched;
- every report from the receiver put off the regular poll (firmware check,
  setup refresh, the HTTP fallback while telnet is down);
- choosing a speaker layout while one was being written was silently
  ignored.

Found in the review of the new receiver support before the release: the
restore order (positions after the layout), a way back that could be lost
on a second change or a change not seen in time, grayed positions counted
as reset, and the subwoofer layout shown in standard mode, where it does
not apply.
