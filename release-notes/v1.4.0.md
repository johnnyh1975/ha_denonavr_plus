# Denon AVR Plus 1.4.0

**Activities that set a zone up in one go, a speech clarity switch, and the
receiver for Assist and AI agents.**

Needs **Home Assistant 2026.8** or newer. After updating, reload the Home
Assistant app or page once. Read
[Upgrading](https://github.com/johnnyh1975/ha_denonavr_plus/blob/main/docs/UPGRADING.md):
there is one new switch.

---

## New

- **Activities** (Configure → Activities): one choice such as *Film* or
  *Music* for a zone. It switches the zone on and sets its input, and if
  you like:
  - the sound mode, the volume (kept below the zone's maximum), the HDMI
    output, the audio delay and speech clarity;
  - switches on the player linked to the input and one more device, such
    as the TV next to a streaming box.

  Each zone with activities gets an **Activity** select. It shows what was
  started last, here, by the new action `denonavr_plus.start_activity` or by
  voice, while the zone stays on that input.
- **Speech clarity**, a switch: the Dialogue Enhancer to medium where the
  receiver has one, otherwise the center level 3 dB up, and Dynamic Volume
  to medium where it was off. Off puts back exactly what it changed, also
  after a restart of Home Assistant or a setting the receiver refused.
- **Voice and AI agents:**
  - New intents to start an activity, set the sound mode and ask why a zone
    is silent (and fix it). Without a zone named, the one that has the
    activity, or the one in the voice satellite's room, is meant. Answers
    come in the language of the request.
  - AI conversation agents with the Assist API get them as tools as soon as
    one of the receiver's zones is exposed.
  - The user guide has sentences in English and German for the built-in
    Assist agent, including *make the voices clearer* for speech clarity.
- **Newer receivers** (AVC-X3800H, X3900H and others of that generation),
  from LaserGuruGuy's recordings:
  - quick select names in their newer form;
  - Auto Lip Sync over `OPALS`;
  - levels of subwoofers 2 to 4 where the receiver reports them;
  - numbers with the receiver's own number of digits.
- A **speaker preset** switch (Speaker preset 1/2) reads the speaker setup
  again: levels, distances, sizes and crossovers follow at once.
- After a **firmware update** the receiver's new version shows on the
  device page and under Updates without restarting Home Assistant.
- The **night mode blueprint** can switch speech clarity on at night and off
  by day. The example dashboard has the activities and speech clarity.

## Changed

- Announcements run in a module of their own with clear steps; nothing
  changes in how they behave.

Found in an independent review before the release:
- the example sentences would also have caught *start a timer* and *start
  the vacuum*; they now need the word *activity* and *sound mode*;
- the select missed activities started by the action or by voice;
- speech clarity forgot what it could not put back, and switching it on
  again would have raised the center twice;
- sound mode, HDMI output, audio delay and speech clarity were kept for
  Zone 2 activities, where they do nothing.

## Tested

- 1005 tests pass on Home Assistant 2026.8 and 2026.10; ruff, strict typing,
  the coverage per module and the repository checks pass. The forms were
  checked in the running interface with a simulated receiver.
- Not yet tried on a receiver:
  - activities and speech clarity on the AVR-X2000 and AVR-X2600H;
  - the Dialogue Enhancer command (`PSDEH`);
  - whether a receiver overrides a sound mode set right after an input
    change;
  - the newer-receiver formats, beyond the recorded AVC-X3900H and
    AVR-X3600H.
